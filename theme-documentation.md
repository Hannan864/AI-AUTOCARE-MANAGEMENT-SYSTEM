# UI/UX Theme Documentation: Dark Minimalist Corporate

This application utilizes a **Dark Minimalist Corporate** design theme, characterized by high-contrast legibility, professional aesthetics, and a focus on essential functionality.

### Design Principles
- **Modern Enterprise Aesthetic**: Utilizes a combination of deep slate backgrounds and bold indigo accents to convey trust and technical sophistication.
- **Minimalist Layouts**: Information is structured in clearly defined, spacious cards and modular components, ensuring a clutter-free user experience.
- **High-Contrast Precision**: Focuses on clean typography and careful use of color to guide user attention to primary actions (e.g., "Sign In").

### Key Styling & Assets
- **Primary Color**: `#6366F1` (Indigo), used for core active states, primary buttons, and navigational highlights.
- **Typography**: `Inter` sans-serif, chosen for its neutral, highly legible quality across enterprise dashboard contexts.
- **Component Styling**:
    - **Capsule Navigation**: Smooth, rounded button and navbar styles creating a modern, fluid interaction feel.
    - **Card Design**: Subtle shadows, distinct background layering, and consistent whitespace to separate content areas.
- **Dark Mode Default**: Designed specifically to reduce eye-strain during extended use sessions typical in vehicle maintenance management.
